---
name: puppeteer-recovery
description: Resume confirmed receipts, check connection, and pause or resume without replaying effects.
---

Use the original receipt with puppeteer_result. Polling never submits a
second coding task. Only awaiting_approval asks the owner to approve in
Latch. Submitted is acceptance, not completion. Return the exact response_text
and actual native answer; do not invent tests, readiness or a finish time.

The group watcher reads receipts after restart. The puppeteer-recover
OpenClaw command job runs every five minutes after configuration, without
a model or scheduler announcement. On an idle workspace it performs no
Plow/Mac calls. Registration reads the current scheduler, reconciles only
its exact job name, and preserves unrelated automations. Listing failure
or corrupt state cannot cause a blind reset or new job.

An interrupted sending state becomes uncertain. Never replay an uncertain
phone send, private question, owner answer or native effect. Status can
retrieve a confirmed result; it cannot establish a delivery the provider
did not confirm. A timed-out native task retains its reservation until the
owner resolves it. The same filesystem locks coordinate the watcher and
maintenance process. A live holder cannot be displaced by a slow service.

Owner-only setup status checks current Mac connection without changing
state or starting workers. Stop pauses new work, cancels queued tasks and
retires this demo's workers while preserving worktrees. Resume keeps the
same grants and workspace; cancelled and uncertain tasks are not replayed.
Respect refusals and retain their receipts. Do not bypass Latch with raw
tools, alternative chats, shell commands or a replacement coder.
